How to play

Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out. Move clockwise around the board, then up your own coloured lane to the centre. You need the exact roll to finish.

Land on an opponent’s pawn to send it back to its yard. Pawns on a star or a start square are safe. A 6, a capture or reaching the centre gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row lose your turn.

Two dice: the host can switch to two dice. Each die is a separate move for any pawn, so pick a die and then a pawn. Doubles, a capture or reaching the centre give you another roll, and three doubles in a row lose your turn. A 6 on a die brings a pawn out.

The server rolls the dice, so nobody can cheat.
