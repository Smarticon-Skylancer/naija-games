How to play

Match the top card by shape or number. Empty your hand first to win. Everyone plays from their own device.

1 Hold on: play again. 2 Pick two. 5 Pick three. 8 Suspension: the next player misses a turn. 14 General market: everyone else picks one, you play again. 20 Whot: play any time and call a shape.

Facing a 2 or a 5? Play the same number to pass the pick on, or pick the cards yourself.
